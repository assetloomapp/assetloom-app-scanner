# AssetLoom App Scanner

Scan your Google Workspace tenant for the third-party apps your users have granted OAuth access to — every app, every permission, every user. See which AI tools are in use and which unknown apps hold dangerous permissions like full Gmail or Drive access. Nothing is stored; nothing leaves your machine except the scan itself.

**Documentation, installation, and setup: [assetloomapp.github.io/assetloom-app-scanner](https://assetloomapp.github.io/assetloom-app-scanner/)**
